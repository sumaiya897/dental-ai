Patients who used to avoid the dentist.

Good questions, straight answers.

Anything else? Call the front desk and a real person will pick up.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Larger treatments can be spread over 6 to 24 months, interest-free, and you get the full price in writing before we start.

Will it hurt?

We numb the area with gel before any injection and pause whenever you ask. Most patients tell us it was easier than they expected.

Can I bring my children?

Absolutely. We see patients from age two and keep a quiet, toy-friendly room for first visits.

Do you accept insurance?

We work with most major providers and handle the claim paperwork for you. Mention your plan when booking.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call before noon and we hold same-day slots for pain, swelling and broken teeth.
